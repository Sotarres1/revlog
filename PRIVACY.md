# RevLog Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

RevLog ("the app") is a vehicle maintenance tracker. This policy explains what
information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email
address and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your password in
readable form.

**Vehicle and maintenance data.** Information you choose to enter, including
vehicle details (make, model, year, trim, VIN, mileage), service records, costs,
reminders, fuel fill-ups, and modifications.

**Photos.** If you add a photo of a vehicle, the image is uploaded to our secure
storage. The app requests access to your photo library only when you tap to add
a photo, and only accesses the specific image you select.

**We do not collect:** location data, contacts, browsing activity, advertising
identifiers, or any analytics or tracking information.

## How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used solely to provide the app's features: storing your
maintenance records, syncing them across your devices, and sending you
reminder notifications you have set up. We do not use your data for advertising,
and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

## Where Your Data Is Stored

Data is stored using Supabase, a cloud database provider, and is protected by
row-level security so that each account can access only its own records.
Transmission between the app and our servers is encrypted.

## Third-Party Services

**NHTSA vPIC (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov).** When you decode a VIN, that VIN is sent to
the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public vehicle database
to retrieve specifications. No account information is included in this request.

**Supabase.** Provides database, authentication, and file storage. See
supabase.com/privacy.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders you create are scheduled locally on your
device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## Your Rights and Choices

**Access and correction.** All of your data is visible and editable within the
app at any time.

**Deletion.** You may permanently delete your account and all associated data
at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account, all
vehicles, service records, reminders, fuel logs, modifications, and photos.
This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

## Children's Privacy

RevLog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with
a revised "Last updated" date.
