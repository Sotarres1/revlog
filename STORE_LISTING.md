## Description (4000 characters max)

```
RevLog is a maintenance log built for people who actually care about their cars.

Log every oil change, brake job, and track day prep. Keep a build sheet of your mods. Watch your MPG and cost per mile. All of it organized by vehicle, synced to the cloud, and available on your phone whenever you're under the hood or at the parts counter.

YOUR GARAGE
• Add every vehicle you own — daily driver, project car, weekend toy
• Decode your VIN to auto-fill year, make, model, trim, engine, horsepower, transmission, and more
• Add your own photos, or let your manufacturer's logo stand in

SERVICE HISTORY
• Log any service with mileage, cost, shop, and notes
• Mark jobs as DIY and track what you've saved doing your own wrenching
• Preset service types for oil, tires, brakes, fluids, plugs, and more
• Complete records when it's time to sell — a documented car is worth more

SMART REMINDERS
• Set reminders by date, by mileage, or both
• Recurring intervals reschedule themselves when you mark a job done
• Get notified before you're overdue instead of after

FUEL & MPG
• Quick fill-up logging with automatic total cost
• Real MPG calculated between full tanks
• See your fuel spend at a glance

BUILD SHEET
• Log every mod with brand, category, cost, and install mileage
• See exactly how much you've invested in the build
• Suspension, wheels, exhaust, intake, tune, and more

STATS THAT MATTER
• Total spend broken down by maintenance, fuel, and mods
• Cost per mile across everything you've tracked
• Six-month spending trend and recent MPG history

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data is yours. No ads, no tracking, no selling your information. Delete your account and everything goes with it, instantly.

Every mile. Every wrench. Logged.
```

## Screenshots Required

Apple requires screenshots for **6.9" iPhone** (1320 x 2868 px). You need 3–10.

Best ones to capture, in order:

1. **Garage** — with 2+ vehicles, at least one with a real photo
2. **Vehicle detail** — showing the specs card from VIN decode + service history
3. **Stats** — the charts screen with real spending data
4. **Build sheet** — a few mods logged
5. **Reminders** — showing an overdue item in red

**How to capture:** open the app on your iPhone, press Volume Up + Side Button
at the same time on each screen. Screenshots land in Photos → AirDrop them to
your Mac. Fill your app with realistic data first — empty screens sell nothing.
